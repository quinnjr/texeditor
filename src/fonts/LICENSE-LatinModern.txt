Latin Modern fonts
==================

The .woff2 files in this directory are subsets of the Latin Modern OpenType
fonts (lmroman10-regular/italic/bold/bolditalic, lmmono10-regular), created
with pyftsubset and restricted to Latin-1 plus typographic punctuation.
